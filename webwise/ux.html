User Experience Design

User Experience Design, or UX Design, focuses on creating websites that are useful, easy to understand, and easy to use.

UX Research Methods

  • Surveys collect feedback from users.
  • Interviews help identify user needs.
  • Usability testing shows how users complete tasks.
  • User personas represent different types of users.
  • Observation can show where users experience problems.

Research Findings

UX research can identify confusing navigation, unclear labels, difficult tasks, or information that users do not understand.

How Research Improves Design

Developers can use research findings to improve navigation, page layouts, buttons, instructions, and other website features.
